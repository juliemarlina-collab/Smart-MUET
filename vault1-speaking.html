Tips & Tricks
PREP Strategy & Task B Interaction
▼
P
Point — State your stand first
The examiner decides your band within your first sentence.
Use: “I believe…” · “In my opinion…” · “I strongly feel that…”
R
Reason — One strong reason only
One strong reason scores more than three weak ones.
Use: “This is because…” · “The reason is…”
E
Example — Malaysian context
Use PTPTN, campus life, cost of living, EPF, local companies.
Use: “For example…” · “Take Malaysia as an example…”
P
Point Again — Close confidently
Signal the examiner you have finished.
Use: “Therefore, I strongly believe…” · “In conclusion…”

👥 TASK B — INTERACTION FUNCTIONS (MPM)

🎯Initiate: “Shall we start by looking at…?”
🗣Prompt: “[Name], what do you think?” · “Do you agree, [name]?”
⚡Turn-take: “Building on what [name] said…” · “I would like to add…”
⚖️Negotiate: “That is a valid point, however…” · “I see it differently…”
🚫Interrupt politely: “Sorry to interrupt, but…” · “May I add something?”
✅Conclude: “So as a group, we have decided the best option is…”

💡 GIVING YOUR REASON (R)
“This is because…” · “The reason is…” · “Due to the fact that…”
“This can be attributed to…” · “One major reason is…”
🏭 ELABORATING (E)
“For example…” · “For instance…” · “A clear example of this is…”
“Take Malaysia as an example…” · “To illustrate this point…”
✅ CONCLUDING (P again)
“Therefore, I strongly believe that…” · “In conclusion…”
“To summarise my point…” · “This is why I feel that…”

Initiate: “Shall we start by looking at…?”

erm… · like… · you know… · so yeah… — replace with a 1-second pause

Sample topic: Salary as reason for choosing a job

P: I believe salary is the most important reason when choosing a job because it directly determines financial security.

R: A good salary allows a graduate to repay education loans, cover living expenses and begin saving — all at once.

E: Many Malaysian graduates with PTPTN loans must prioritise salary because repayment starts six months after graduation. A salary below RM2,000 in KL makes this extremely difficult.

P: Therefore, salary is the most critical factor as it forms the foundation of financial independence.
📝 Examiner note: Direct answer · one clear reason · specific local example · strong closing. About 90 seconds. No fillers. This response demonstrates all 4 language functions: expressing opinions, giving reasons, elaborating, concluding.
