Your 6-step guide

1Know the test
Task A is an individual presentation. Task B is a group discussion. Focus on clear task fulfilment, relevant ideas and natural interaction.
2Use PREP
Point → Reason → Example → Point again. For Task A, one clear PREP cycle is enough. For Task B, use shorter mini-PREP turns.
3Task A strategy
Use preparation time for keywords only. Start with a direct stand, explain one reason, give one specific example, and close clearly.
4Task B strategy
Listen actively, build on another candidate's idea, invite others to respond, disagree politely and help the group reach a conclusion.
5Language support
Useful stems: In my opinion… / This is because… / For example… / Building on that point… / I agree to some extent, however… / Therefore…
6Exam-day reminder
Do not memorise a full script. Speak to the task, keep eye contact, avoid fillers and use a brief pause when thinking.
