Tips & Tricks
PREP Strategy Before You Practise
▼
P
Point — State your stand
Begin with one clear opinion. Example: “I believe students should work part-time because…”
Start strong. The examiner listens to your opening sentence first.
R
Reason — Explain why
Use: because, the reason is, this is because, due to the fact that
Give one strong reason — do not list many weak ones.
E
Example — Malaysian context
Use: for example, for instance, a clear example of this is…
Local examples score better: PTPTN, campus life, Malaysian companies, EPF.
P
Point Again — Close confidently
Restate your stand: “Therefore, I strongly believe that…”
This signals the examiner that you have completed your response.
⏱️ Task B reminder: Write 3–4 keywords only during prep time. Agree politely, add one reason, invite others by name, and help the group reach a final decision in the last 2 minutes.

SAMPLE TOPIC: MAKING NEW FRIENDS AS AN ADVANTAGE OF HOSTEL ACTIVITIES
P
Point
R
Reason
E
Example
P
Point Again
P — I believe making new friends is the most valuable advantage of hostel activities because it helps students build a support network away from home.

R — This is because hostel activities such as game nights and communal events bring students from different backgrounds together, encouraging natural interaction and friendship.

E — For example, many first-year students say that joining orientation-week icebreaker games at their hostel helped them find close friends who later became their study partners.

P — Therefore, making new friends is a key advantage as it gives students both emotional support and a stronger sense of belonging throughout their time at university.
📝 Examiner note: Direct answer · one clear reason · specific relatable example · strong closing. About 90 seconds.

Useful Speaking Phrases
Ready-Made Phrases for Task A & Task B
▼
🥳 STATING YOUR POINT (P)
“I believe that…” · “In my opinion…” · “I strongly feel that…”
“From my perspective…” · “I would argue that…”
💡 GIVING YOUR REASON (R)
“This is because…” · “The reason is…” · “Due to the fact that…”
“This can be attributed to…” · “One major reason is…”
🏭 GIVING AN EXAMPLE (E)
“For example…” · “For instance…” · “A clear example of this is…”
“Take Malaysia as an example…” · “To illustrate this point…”
👥 TASK B — INTERACTION PHRASES (MPM FUNCTIONS)
Initiate: “Shall we start by looking at…?”
Prompt: “What do you think, [name]?” · “[Name], do you agree?”
Turn-take: “Building on what [name] said…” · “I would like to add…”
Negotiate: “That is a valid point, however…” · “I see it differently because…”
Interrupt: “May I add something?” · “Sorry to interrupt, but…”
Conclude: “So as a group, we have decided the best option is…”
🚫 AVOID THESE FILLER WORDS
❌ erm… · ❌ like… · ❌ you know… · ❌ so yeah…
Replace with a 1-second pause. A pause sounds confident. Fillers sound nervous.
