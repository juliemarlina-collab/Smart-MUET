Tips & Technique
SCAN strategy before you practise
▾
SStudy
CCircle
AAnalyse
NNarrow
S — Study: Read the question before the passage
Know exactly what you are looking for before you start reading — a name, a reason, an opinion, a comparison.
C — Circle: Identify the keywords to scan for
Pick out the specific noun, number or phrase in the question that you can search for in the passage.
A — Analyse: Read closely around the keyword
Once you locate the keyword, read the full sentence (and the one before/after) carefully — the answer is rarely just the keyword itself.
N — Narrow: Eliminate options the text does not support
Cross out any option that isn't directly backed by the passage, then pick the one that is — even if another option sounds true in real life.

⚠️ COMMON DISTRACTOR PATTERNS

🔁Word match trap: an option repeats a word from the text but changes its meaning.
🌍Outside knowledge trap: an option is true in real life but is never actually stated in the passage.
⚖️Absolute word trap: words like "always", "never" or "all" are often too extreme to be supported by the text.
📍Off-topic detail: a fact that is true and in the text, but does not answer the actual question asked.

Quick Reminders

🎯Study the question before searching the passage for evidence
📝Choose the answer supported by the text, not outside knowledge
⏱️Complete easier items first and return to difficult questions later
🚫Do not choose an option only because it repeats words from the text

Sample question: Part 1, Q1 — Which place has the cheapest wi-fi?

S — StudyThe question asks you to compare one specific detail — wi-fi cost — across three places (A, B and C).
C — CircleKeywords to scan for: "cheapest" and "wi-fi".
A — AnalyseScanning each text: Place A charges RM10/day for wi-fi, Place B charges RM20/day, Place C charges RM20/day.
N — NarrowRM10 is lower than RM20, so Place B and Place C are eliminated. Place A is the cheapest — the answer is A.
📝 EXAMINER NOTE

Answer: A. Notice the question never says "RM10" — that number only appears in the text. This is why circling the keyword first, then analysing the exact sentence it appears in, is the reliable way to find the answer.

Language Support
Signal words to look for in the text
▾
🔄 CONTRAST & CONCESSION SIGNALS
"however…" · "although…" · "on the other hand…" · "despite…"
"even though…" · "in contrast…"
➡️ CAUSE & EFFECT SIGNALS
"because…" · "as a result…" · "therefore…" · "due to…"
"this led to…" · "consequently…"
❗ EMPHASIS SIGNALS
"notably…" · "in particular…" · "above all…" · "most importantly…"
"significantly…"
🔗 SEQUENCE & DISCOURSE SIGNALS
"firstly…" · "meanwhile…" · "subsequently…" · "in conclusion…"
"since then…" · "eventually…"
👉 REFERENCE WORD TRAPS
Pronouns like "this", "it", "these" and "which" — always check exactly what they refer back to before answering.
❌ DON'T BE FOOLED BY
Options that use exact words from the text but change their meaning · absolute words ("always", "never", "all", "none") that overstate what the text says · opinions mistaken for facts (or facts mistaken for the writer's opinion).
Choose only what the passage actually supports.
