Your 6-step guide

1Know the paper
Reading rewards accurate retrieval, paraphrase recognition, inference and time control. Do not read every text slowly from line one.
2Question-first strategy
Read the question and key options first. Identify what information you need before returning to the passage.
3Scan efficiently
Scan names, dates, numbers, repeated ideas and synonyms. Match meaning, not only identical words.
4Handle inference
Ask: what does the writer imply based on the evidence? Avoid answers that are true generally but not supported by the text.
5Control time
Move on when stuck. Mark uncertain items and return later. Protect enough time for the final parts.
6Exam-day reminder
Use evidence from the text. Do not choose an option simply because one familiar keyword appears.
