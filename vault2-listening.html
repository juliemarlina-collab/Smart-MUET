Keyword Notes Required

Write short keywords as you listen. 2–3 words per line maximum.

🚦 SIGNAL WORDS TO WATCH
CORRECTION SIGNALS — second answer is correct
“actually…” · “but in fact…” · “however…” · “I mean…” · “sorry, I meant…” · “to correct myself…”
EXAMPLE
“The meeting was on Thursday — sorry, I mean Friday.” → Answer = Friday
⚠️ Wait for the speaker to FINISH the complete thought before writing your answer.

Listening Practice Guide — K.E.Y.

K
Keywords
Write short notes only: names, dates, places, numbers and repeated words.
E
Expect Distractors
The first answer you hear may change. Wait for correction words like but, actually, or instead.
Y
Yield Final Answer
Choose the answer after listening to the full idea, not just one matching word.
Before submitting: check every question has an answer and compare it with your keyword notes.
