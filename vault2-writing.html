Situation:

Your coursemate, Linda, attended a talk on online business last week. Read her email and write a reply using all the notes provided.
  • Agree that online business is becoming more popular than traditional business.
  • Agree to join Linda in setting up an online business.
  • Suggest two products you could sell.
  • Explain why you cannot start immediately and suggest a better time.
Need Task 1 language support?

Open: “Thanks for telling me about the talk…”
Agree: “I completely agree that…”
Suggest: “We could consider selling…”
Delay: “I don’t think we should start immediately because…”
Close: “Let me know what you think.”

Statement:

“The lack of appreciation for our culture has caused Malaysians to lose their true identity.”

Write an essay expressing your opinion on the statement.
Need PEEL support?

Use PEEL for your body paragraphs only:
P Point → E Explain → E Example → L Link.

Your introduction should state your stand clearly, and your conclusion should return to the question.

Need essay language support?

Position: “I strongly believe that…” / “I partially agree that…”
Add: “Furthermore…” / “In addition…”
Contrast: “However…” / “On the other hand…”
Conclude: “In conclusion…” / “Therefore, it is evident that…”
