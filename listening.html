Tips & Technique
HEAR strategy before you practise
▾
HHighlight
EExpect
AAttend
RReview
H — Highlight: Mark keywords before the audio starts
Skim each question in the seconds before playback and underline the key noun or verb. That is what your ears are listening for.
E — Expect: Predict the type of answer
Decide if you are listening for a name, a number, a reason or a feeling. This narrows what you tune in for.
A — Attend: Catch signal words as they happen
Words like "however", "actually" or "I mean" often flag a correction — the answer usually follows the correction, not the first thing said.
R — Review: Check evidence before you commit
Match what you actually heard against each option — do not pick an option just because it repeats a word you recognised.

⚠️ COMMON DISTRACTOR PATTERNS

🔁Word match trap: an option repeats a word from the audio but changes its meaning.
↩️Self-correction: the speaker says one thing, then corrects it — the correction is the answer.
🔢Number mix-up: similar-sounding numbers and letters (13/30, b/d) are common distractors.
🙅Negation flip: a small "not" or "never" changes the whole meaning of a sentence.

Quick Reminders

🎯You only hear each part once — there's no rewind in the real exam
📝Predict the answer type before the audio starts playing
🔢Take extra care with numbers, spelling and similar-sounding names
🚫Do not choose an option just because it repeats familiar words

Sample question: Part I, Q1 — Leanne is happy because she can

H — HighlightKeywords in the question: "happy" and "can" — you are listening for the reason Leanne feels happy.
E — ExpectThe answer type is an action or reason — expect a verb phrase like "bring… to school" or "be in charge of…".
A — AttendIn the audio, Leanne says: "I'm so happy — now I can finally bring my pet tortoise to school for show-and-tell!" That is the clue.
R — ReviewOption A ("bring her pet to school") matches exactly what was said. Options B and C mention clubs and Amir, which are not the reason given.
📝 EXAMINER NOTE

Answer: A. Notice the question never uses the word "tortoise" or "show-and-tell" — those only appear in the audio. This is why highlighting keywords first, then listening for their meaning rather than their exact wording, is the reliable way to find the answer.

Signal Word Bank
Signal words to listen for
▾
🔄 CONTRAST & CORRECTION SIGNALS
"however…" · "actually…" · "I mean…" · "sorry, let me rephrase…"
"on the other hand…" · "that's not quite right…"
➡️ SEQUENCE SIGNALS
"first…" · "then…" · "after that…" · "meanwhile…"
"before…" · "finally…" · "in the end…"
❗ EMPHASIS & IMPORTANCE SIGNALS
"the most important…" · "especially…" · "in particular…"
"above all…" · "what really matters is…"
💭 OPINION & ATTITUDE SIGNALS
"I believe…" · "I think…" · "in my opinion…" · "honestly…"
"to be fair…" · "surprisingly…" · "I was worried that…"
🔢 NUMBER & SPELLING TRAPS
Numbers said quickly (13 vs. 30) · similar letters (B/D, M/N)
Approximations: "about…" · "nearly…" · "just over…" · "roughly…"
❌ DON'T BE FOOLED BY
Options that repeat exact words from the audio but change their meaning · options mentioned early then corrected · options that sound plausible but were never actually said.
Choose only what the recording supports.
