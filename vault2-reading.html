⚠️ COMMON MISTAKES — AVOID THESE
❌ Choosing an answer because the WORDS appear in the passage
Wrong — matching words is a distractor trap. The correct answer paraphrases the meaning, not the exact words.
❌ Reading the entire passage before looking at the questions
Wrong — read questions FIRST so you know what to scan for. Full reading wastes time.
❌ Trusting your general knowledge for inference questions
Wrong — all answers must be supported by the TEXT. What you know outside the passage does not count.
❌ Spending too long on one difficult question
Wrong — skip and return. Every question is 1 mark. Losing 5 minutes on one question costs you elsewhere.

📚 VOCABULARY BOOSTER — KEY READING WORDS

These words often appear in MUET Reading passages. Understanding them helps you find the right answer faster.

imply / suggest
hint at without stating directly → inference question signal
attribute / cause
assign a reason to something → cause-effect questions
despite / although
contrast signals → opposite meaning in options
moreover / furthermore
adding more information → cohesion questions
consequently / therefore
result/effect signals → conclusion questions
paraphrase / rephrase
say the same thing differently → the correct answer always paraphrases

Reading Practice Guide — S.C.A.N.

S
Skim
Read quickly to understand the main idea of the passage.
C
Check Questions
Read the question stem first so you know what information to find.
A
Analyse Options
Compare all options carefully. Do not choose an answer only because it has similar words.
N
Negate Distractors
Eliminate answers that are too broad, too narrow, opposite or not stated.
