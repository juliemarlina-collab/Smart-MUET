Your 6-step guide

1Know the tasks
Task 1 requires a focused response to the given situation. Task 2 requires a clear position developed as an extended essay.
2Plan before writing
Spend a few minutes deciding your position, main points and examples. A short plan prevents repetition and weak organisation.
3Use PEEL
Point → Explain → Example → Link. One strong paragraph should develop one clear controlling idea.
4Task fulfilment first
Answer exactly what the prompt asks. Good English cannot fully rescue a response that ignores important requirements.
5Language control
Use varied sentence structures and accurate vocabulary. Choose precise language over unnecessarily difficult words.
6Exam-day reminder
Leave time to check word count, paragraphing, subject–verb agreement, tense, spelling and whether every required point was addressed.
