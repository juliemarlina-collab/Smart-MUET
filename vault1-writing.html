Writing Practice Guide — P.E.E.L.

P
Point
Write the main idea clearly at the start of the paragraph.
E
Explain
Explain your point using clear reasoning. Use because, therefore, or this means that.
E
Example
Support your idea with a relevant example, situation or evidence.
L
Link
Link the paragraph back to the question or your opinion.
