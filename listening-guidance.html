Your 6-step guide

1Before audio starts
Read the question quickly. Predict the type of answer you need: person, reason, place, number, opinion or action.
2Listen for signals
Pay attention to contrast and correction words such as but, however, actually, instead and finally.
3Use short notes
Write keywords and abbreviations. Full sentences take too long and make you miss the next point.
4Watch distractors
The first answer mentioned may later be corrected. Wait for the speaker's final meaning before deciding.
5Recover quickly
If you miss one item, move on. Do not sacrifice the next two questions while thinking about the previous one.
6Exam-day reminder
Keep your eyes on the next item and stay calm. Listening performance drops quickly when attention remains on a missed answer.
